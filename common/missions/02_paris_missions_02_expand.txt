# =========================================================================
# BRANCH A: METROPOLITAN PARIS & AGRARIAN HINTERLAND
# =========================================================================
# Focus: Transforming the insular river oppidum into the premier Western
# European metropolis, expanding across both riverbanks, building permanent
# bridge infrastructure, and planting orchards in the countryside.

# TASK A1: Metropolis of the Sekuana (Urban Capstone)
# Description:
#   Expand Lutetia beyond a fortified tribal town into a grand cultural and
#   commercial metropolis. Requires high population (40-50+ pops), civic
#   buildings (forum, aqueduct, academy/library, tax office, theatre).
# Reward:
#   Set city status to metropolis; permanent province modifier:
#   "Gallic Metropolis of Lutetia" (+civ value, +commerce value, +pop capacity,
#   +local migration attraction).

# TASK A2: Bridges of the Two Arms (Engineering Milestone)
# Description:
#   Span both arms of the Seine (Petit Bras to the south, Grand Bras to the north)
#   with durable timber and stone bridge crossings. This permanently unifies
#   the island fortress with both banks and solidifies total control over
#   cross-river overland transit between Northern and Southern Gaul.
# Requirements:
#   Owns 2475, treasury >= 150, stone surplus or quarry network developed.
# Reward:
#   Local modifier "Bridges of the Sekuana" (+local trade routes, +movement speed,
#   +defensive advantage against river crossings).

# TASK A3: The Southern Suburb (Latin Quarter Antecedent)
# Description:
#   Settle craft guilds, artisans, and merchant clans on the Left Bank rising
#   up Mount Sainte-Geneviève. Establishes residential quarters and market
#   districts outside the congested island walls.
# Requirements:
#   Pop in 2475 >= 35, built forum/marketplace.
# Reward:
#   Spawns citizen and freemen pops; boosts freeman output and tech investment.

# TASK A4: Grand River Quays of the Nautae
# Description:
#   Construct stone-embanked wharves and deep-water river moorings along the
#   banks of Lutetia, replacing timber docks with permanent riverfront quays
#   capable of handling deep-draft river barges and trade fleets.
# Requirements:
#   Nautae agreement honored, port building in 2475, wood and stone supplies.
# Reward:
#   Modifier "Grand Quays of the Nautae" (+river commerce, +shipbuilding speed,
#   +national export tariff bonus).

# TASK A5: Parisian Orchards & Agricultural Estates (Market Gardens)
# Description:
#   As the metropolitan population surges, grain alone is insufficient to
#   feed the diverse urban market. The fertile silt along the river bends
#   is cleared and planted with fruit orchards and vegetable gardens.
# Historical Note:
#   Transitioning an existing redundant rural good (or suburban Paris tile)
#   into "fruits" or "vegetables" trade good, accompanied by slave estates /
#   latifundia.
# Requirements:
#   Lutetia population >= 40, owns target Seine farmland tile, agricultural estate
#   or farming settlement built.
# Reward:
#   Changes trade good to fruits; adds provincial food capacity modifier:
#   "Orchards of the Sekuana" (+local food production, +pop growth).

# TASK A6: Pillar of the Boatmen (Monumental Architecture)
# Description:
#   Erect a colossal monumental column/temple in the heart of Lutetia, sculpted
#   by native and foreign artisans to honor Cernunnos, Smertrios, Esus, and
#   Tarvos Trigaranus alongside syncretic deities of commerce and water.
# Requirements:
#   Holy site established in Tree 1 (me_parisii.25), high piety/stability, gold.
# Reward:
#   Permanent holy site modifier, +national pantheon deity power, +religious unity.


# =========================================================================
# BRANCH B: THE ARMORICAN QUESTION ("THE CARTHAGE OF GAUL")
# =========================================================================
# Focus: Geopolitical competition with the maritime tribes of Armorica
# (Veneti, Osismii, Redones) who dominate the Atlantic cabotage and tin trade
# from the Cassiterides / Britannia.
# Structure: Offers a strategic fork between mercantile accommodation and
# total naval conquest.

# --- FORK 1: MERCANTILE ACCORD (The Diplomatic Path) ---
# TASK B1-A: The Armorican Trade Concordat
# Description:
#   Negotiate a formal treaty with the maritime council of the Veneti.
#   Establish shared trade spheres: the Parisii control the riverine interior
#   and continental markets, while Armorican ships carry British tin and
#   Atlantic salt into the Seine estuary.
# Requirements:
#   Alliance or high positive relations (+80) with lead Armorican power (or Veneti),
#   diplomatic reputation >= 2, trade route active with Armorica.
# Reward:
#   National modifier: "Atlantic-Seine Trade League" (+commerce income,
#   +import routes, +naval range, reduced diplomatic annexation cost for maritime allies).

# --- FORK 2: SEINE NAVAL DOMINANCE (The Militant Path) ---
# TASK B1-B: Challenge the Ocean Masters
# Description:
#   Build a permanent war fleet on the Lower Seine and challenge Armorican
#   naval supremacy. Seize the Atlantic ports by force to establish an unbroken
#   monopoly over all cross-channel commerce.
# Requirements:
#   Owns Seine estuary (2471 / Veliocassi coast), naval force >= 20 ships,
#   military experience >= 30.
# Reward:
#   Grants claims on all of Armorica (Aremorica Region / Venetia Area);
#   temporary naval combat modifier: "Sekuana Shipwrights" (+galley combat ability,
#   +naval morale).

# TASK B2-B: Subjugation of the Veneti
# Description:
#   Capture the tidal fortresses and shipyards of the Veneti. Break the power
#   of the maritime elders and integrate their Atlantic fleets into our realm.
# Requirements:
#   Direct or subject ownership of Darioritum and Armorican coastal centers.
# Reward:
#   Seizes Armorican trade ships; establishes provincial modifier "Armorican Shipyards"
#   (+trireme/octere ship build speed, +coastal commerce).


# =========================================================================
# BRANCH C: BELGICA PACIFICATION & NORTHERN FRONTIER
# =========================================================================
# Focus: Securing the northern frontier along the Somme and Oise watersheds;
# dealing with the warlike Belgic tribes (Remi, Bellovaci, Suessiones, Nervii).
# Incorporates rural production flavor inspired by the Eburones tree.

# TASK C1: Fortify the Somme Frontier
# Description:
#   Construct frontier fortresses and palisades along the natural boundary of
#   the Somme river to deter Belgic raids and protect Seine basin agriculture.
# Requirements:
#   Control border territories along northern Belgica; at least 2 forts constructed.
# Reward:
#   Defensive frontier modifier; +fort defense, +garrison size on northern border.

# TASK C2: Belgic Artisanry & Trade Goods (Eburones-Style Rural Flavor)
# Description:
#   Harness the distinctive production capabilities of the Belgic marches:
#   Belgic woad dyes, forest honey, and northern cattle breeds.
# Requirements:
#   Owns relevant Belgic tiles with slave estates / rural investments.
# Reward:
#   Tile-specific production modifiers:
#   - "Belgic Woad Vats" (+dyes goods from pops, +commerce value)
#   - "Northern Apiaries" (+honey production, +local food output)
#   - "Ardennes Timber Camps" (+wood production, reduced building costs)

# TASK C3: The Belgic Confederation Pact
# Description:
#   Bring the Belgic chieftains into client tribal league status or permanent
#   vassalage, securing our northern flank through treaties of mutual defense.
# Requirements:
#   Belgic tribes either subject vassals or conquered; stability >= 50.
# Reward:
#   Feudatory integration speed bonus; integrates Belgic culture pops as citizens/freemen.


# =========================================================================
# BRANCH D: THE SOUTHERN AXIS & MEDITERRANEAN DIPLOMACY
# =========================================================================
# Focus: Pushing south past Alesia into the Saone and Rhone corridors;
# direct diplomatic and commercial relations with Massalia and Hellenic trade.

# TASK D1: Beyond the Lingones (Saone Watershed)
# Description:
#   Push down from the Upper Seine into the fertile Saone river valley,
#   securing the strategic river portages connecting the Atlantic drainage
#   to the Mediterranean watershed.
# Requirements:
#   Controls Lingonia, Mandubia (Alesia), and passes into Sequania.
# Reward:
#   Grants claims across the Saone and Upper Rhone corridors; +caravan trade routes.

# TASK D2: The Massalian Embassy (Mediterranean Direct Accord)
# Description:
#   Send a formal state embassy to the Greek phokian colony of Massalia.
#   Expand the initial slave-for-wine trade established in Tree 1 into a
#   permanent mercantile treaty, welcoming Greek scholars, coin-engravers,
#   and vintners to Lutetia.
# Requirements:
#   Has diplomatic range to Massalia (c:MSL); Massalia exists; positive relations.
# Reward:
#   Event: Massalian Treaty of Commerce and Friendship:
#   - Option A: Welcome Greek Merchants (Spawns Hellenic citizen pops in Lutetia,
#     +trade routes, +civ tech growth).
#   - Option B: Demand Preferential Tariffs (+national commerce income, +wine trade value).
#   - Option C: Establish Joint Trade Factoriage (+treasury, +diplomatic rep).
